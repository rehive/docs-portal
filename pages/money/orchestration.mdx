# Orchestration

Orchestration routes across providers so you integrate once and Rehive picks the
right one. It spans **custody providers** (Bridge, Privy, your own) and **payment
providers** (the rails behind deposits, off-ramps and on-chain sends). KYC
providers (SumSub, Sardine) are orchestrated the same way under
[Identity](/identity).

It optimises three things:

- **Access**: reach a provider, rail or region you aren't directly integrated with.
- **Cost**: route a given movement to the cheapest viable provider.
- **Failover**: when one provider is down or declines, fall back to another automatically.

## The default solution flow

Virtual bank account → **Wallet** (Rehive mirrors the balance) → **Custody**
(Bridge or Privy), with Bridge converting USD↔USDC. Orchestration is what wires
that flow together: it provisions the funding method, mirrors the balance on the
Wallet, and hands the underlying value to custody.

## Pairs with Coverage

Orchestration is the routing engine. **[Coverage](/coverage)** is the view of
what's available: the rails, regions and methods in scope right now. Together
they answer "what can I do, and who runs it" without you tracking providers
yourself.

> Orchestration is a cross-cutting capability under both Money and Identity. It
> is not sold standalone. It only makes sense paired with the products it routes
> for.
