# Money

Hold value and move it across rails, without building a ledger. Money is three
things working together: **Wallets** hold, **Payments** move, and
**Orchestration** routes across providers.

A **Wallet** is a balance the customer owns, multi-asset, that **Rehive mirrors
on its ledger**. We record the balance, we never hold the funds. Rehive is not a
bank, custodian or MSB. Underneath the Wallet sits **custody** held by Bridge
(custodial USDC under their license), Privy (end-user self-custody) or your own.

## The default solution flow

Virtual bank account → **Wallet** (Rehive mirrors the balance) → **Custody**
(Bridge or Privy), with Bridge converting USD↔USDC. Money lands in a virtual
bank account, the Wallet reflects it, and custody holds the underlying value.

## What's inside

- **[Wallets](/money/wallets)**: the Wallet object. A mirrored balance, any asset, any custody, ledger optional. Works standalone.
- **[Payments](/money/payments)**: money in, out and between. Virtual bank accounts, on-chain addresses, cards, off-ramps, transfers, payouts and conversions.
- **[Orchestration](/money/orchestration)**: route across custody and payment providers for access, cost and failover.
- **[Add-ons](/money/add-ons)**: Commercial and Engage. Fees, subscriptions, commerce, rewards, notifications, support.
