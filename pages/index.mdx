# Rehive Docs

> **First-stab prototype.** This documentation maps the new product structure
> (Identity · Accounts · Compliance). The APIs shown are a product-design
> proposal, not a shipped contract.

Rehive is the **operating layer for stablecoin finance** — the compliance,
accounts and onboarding a regulated program runs on, above any money-movement
provider.

You can run the whole platform, or adopt one product on its own.

## Three products

| Product | What it does | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| **[Identity](/identity)** | Onboard customers and manage their status before they hit the rails — hosted onboarding, provider-required data, status across providers. | [Identity overview](/identity) |
| **[Accounts](/accounts)** | Programmable accounts: hold balances, fund, transfer and pay out across rails — any asset, any custody. | [Accounts overview](/accounts) |
| **[Compliance](/compliance)** | Policies, limits, controls, monitoring and audit-ready evidence — on any ledger. | [Compliance overview](/compliance) |

Each product is the same shape: **our opinionated layer over a commodity
provider layer.** They compose — Identity → Accounts → Compliance — but each is
adoptable on its own.

## How the docs are organised

- **[Get started](/get-started)** — the model, authentication and a five-minute quickstart.
- **Identity · Accounts · Compliance** — one section per product: overview, capabilities, API reference.
- **[Coverage](/coverage)** — the rails, regions and methods we support.
- **[Advanced](/advanced)** — the low-level platform API, for teams that need it.

## Principles

- **Opinionated, in your terms.** You work with *customers*, *accounts*,
  *modes*, *transfers* — never ledger internals. There are no account
  definitions, subtypes or currency setup to configure.
- **Composable.** Adopt one product, or all three. Bring your own identity,
  ledger or rails on the seams.
- **One API surface.** The product APIs are the front door; the raw platform
  primitives are gated behind [Advanced](/advanced).
