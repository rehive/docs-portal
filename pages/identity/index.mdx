# Identity

Onboard customers and manage their status **before they hit a provider**.
Identity is the layer you'd otherwise build by hand — it collects exactly what
the rails require and hands it off in the shape they expect.

## Why it exists

Money-movement providers like Bridge expect rich data — **source of funds,
purpose of account, occupation, expected activity** — but give you no API or UI
to collect it. Identity is that collection-and-status layer, pre-built.

## What's inside

- **Built-in Auth** — register, sign-in, password reset and MFA, so customers have an identity before they have a Wallet.
- **KYC orchestration** — route across providers (SumSub, Sardine) to collect the right data at optimised cost, with one status out the back.
- **[Hosted onboarding & data collection](/identity/onboarding)** — branded flows that capture KYC and provider-required data, with the right fields and validation.
- **[Customer status](/identity/customer-status)** — one status per customer, tracked across Bridge, Hifi and your other providers.
- Notifications & chat to fill the gaps — request missing info without leaving the platform.
- Routine collection — re-verification, refreshed details and expiring documents.

## Use it alone, or compose it

- **Standalone** — onboard and manage customer status for your own flow.
- **+ [Money](/money)** — customers arrive ready to transact on the rails.
- **+ [Compliance](/compliance)** — collected data and status feed your controls and evidence.
