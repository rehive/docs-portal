# Monitoring

Watch activity, raise alerts, and route cases to your compliance team — with an
audit trail behind every decision.

```
Rules ──▶ Alerts ──▶ Reviews ──▶ Outcome (cleared / escalated / blocked)
```

- **Rules** — conditions over customer, account and transaction attributes (the same condition engine as [controls](/compliance/controls)).
- **Alerts** — raised when a rule matches; can hold a transfer pending review.
- **Reviews** — a queue for your team, with the case, the trigger and the linked evidence.

Every outbound transfer is screened (sanctions / address risk) and every account
is monitored for patterns. Outcomes are recorded as [evidence](/compliance/evidence).
