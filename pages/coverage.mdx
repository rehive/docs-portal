# Coverage

Coverage isn't a product — it's a view of the rail layer, with one source of
truth: the **rails catalogue** (`GET /rails`). The marketing site and these docs
render from the same catalogue, so coverage is never stale.

## Move money

| ACH | US | USD | In · Out |
| Wire / Fedwire | US | USD | In · Out |
| SEPA | EU | EUR | In · Out |
| SWIFT | Global | USD · EUR | Out |
| Stablecoin (on-chain) | Global | USDC · USDT | In · Out |

curl "https://api.rehive.com/v1/rails?currency=USD&direction=out" \
