# Coverage

Coverage isn't a product. It's the view that pairs with
[Orchestration](/money/orchestration): what's available to route across right
now. It has one source of truth: the **rails catalogue** (`GET /rails`) on the
Money API. The marketing site and these docs render from the same catalogue, so
coverage is never stale.

## Move money

| Method | Regions | Currencies | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACH | US | USD | In · Out |
| Wire / Fedwire | US | USD | In · Out |
| SEPA | EU | EUR | In · Out |
| SWIFT | Global | USD · EUR | Out |
| Stablecoin (on-chain) | Global | USDC · USDT | In · Out |
| Cards | Select | USD | Out |

```sh
curl "https://api.rehive.com/v1/rails?currency=USD&direction=out" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_..."
```
