# Advanced

> The product APIs (Identity · Money · Compliance) are the front door, and
> cover the vast majority of programs. This section is the **escape hatch**, not
> the default path.

## Platform API

Underneath, Rehive runs a configurable multi-asset platform. The product APIs
are an opinionated façade over it: they pre-decide the ledger configuration so
you never touch account definitions, subtypes or currency setup.

A small number of advanced and existing integrations need the raw primitives
directly. That access is **gated** and available by arrangement.

## Modes (internal)

Wallets used to be opened in a "mode": a purpose-built preset deciding
currencies, movements, limits and rails. Modes still exist here as an internal
platform mechanism, but they are **not a customer concept** anymore. Customers
never pick a mode, and the product APIs decide the preset for you.

- If a mode plus scoped `overrides` covers your case, stay on the product API.
- If you genuinely need the low-level platform, talk to us about Platform API access.

This keeps the opinionation enforceable for everyone else, and the
configuration surface out of the default developer experience.
