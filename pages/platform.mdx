# Platform

The full solution. Identity, Money and Compliance, pre-integrated and surfaced in
your branded apps, with orchestration across providers underneath. Run the whole
program on Rehive, or adopt one product and grow into the rest.

## The stack

```
Your branded apps        web · mobile · admin · merchant   (or your own, via API)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Rehive operating layer   Identity · Money · Compliance
  Orchestration          one integration, every provider
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Providers (the rails)    Bridge · Privy · Hifi · SumSub · Sardine
```

You build on the operating layer. Rehive runs the program; providers move and
custody the money underneath.

## How it all works together

One customer journey, end to end:

1. **Onboard with Identity.** The customer registers (built-in Auth). Identity
   collects exactly what the rails require and tracks status across providers, so
   they are cleared before they transact.
2. **Hold and move with Money.** A Wallet holds the balance (Rehive mirrors it;
   custody sits underneath). Payments move value in, out and between, over the
   right rail.
3. **Controlled by Compliance.** Every payment passes limits, screening and
   monitoring. Evidence is recorded per control for audits and provider reviews.
4. **Orchestrated underneath.** Orchestration routes across custody, payment and
   KYC providers for access, cost and failover. [Coverage](/coverage) shows what
   is live where.
5. **Surfaced in your apps.** It all shows up in your branded web, mobile, admin
   and merchant apps, or your own front end via the API.

### A payment, end to end

```
Customer deposits USD
   → Identity        cleared for this rail
   → Money           a virtual account funds the Wallet; Rehive mirrors the balance
   → Compliance      limits and screening pass; evidence recorded
   → Orchestration   Bridge converts USD to USDC; best rail chosen
   → Apps            balance and receipt shown; customer notified
```

A blocked payment stops at Compliance: the Wallet shows it held for review and the
case lands in your admin app. Nothing settles until a control clears it.

## Adopt one, or all

Each product stands on its own, so you can start with the one you need and compose
the rest when you are ready. A self-custody Wallet with no Payments or Identity is
a valid starting point; so is Compliance on a ledger you already run. The value
compounds as you add the rest.

See it end to end in the [global dollar account guide](/guides/global-dollar-account):
one fintech taking a customer from sign-up to a funded USD wallet and a
cross-border payout.
