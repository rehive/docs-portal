# The model

The whole API is built from a small set of objects, all in plain product terms.

```
Customer
└─ Wallet             a balance the customer owns, mirrored on Rehive's ledger
   ├─ Balance         the held value, per asset (available / total)
   └─ custody         underneath: Bridge, Privy or your own holds the value
Payment               one money movement, over a Rail (subsumes Transfer / Payout)
Rail                  the route a Payment takes (method × region × currency)
Event                 emitted for every state change

Orchestration         cross-cutting: routes Payments and custody across providers
Policy / Control      Compliance: rules that govern what a Wallet or Customer may do
```

A **Customer** has one or more **Wallets**. A **Wallet** is a balance the
customer owns, multi-asset, that Rehive mirrors on its ledger. The value itself
sits in **custody** underneath. A **Payment** is the single primitive for moving
value, over a **Rail**. **Orchestration** routes payments and custody across
providers. **Events** fire on every change.

| Object | What it is | Product |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | A person or business, with onboarding state and verification. | Identity |
| **Wallet** | A balance the customer owns. Multi-asset, mirrored on Rehive's ledger, with custody underneath. | Money |
| **Balance** | The held value in a single asset within a Wallet (`available` / `total`). | Money |
| **Payment** | The single primitive for moving value: deposit, withdraw, send, receive, convert. Transfer and Payout are sub-types. | Money |
| **Payout** | A batch of outbound payments from one Wallet (a Payment sub-type). | Money |
| **Rail** | A money-movement route (method × region × currency). Read-only catalogue. | Money / Coverage |
| **Orchestration** | Routes payments, custody and KYC across providers for access, cost and failover. | Identity / Money |
| **Policy / Control** | A rule that governs what a Wallet or customer may do. | Compliance |
| **Event** | A state change, delivered to your webhooks. | All |

## A note on terminology

If you've integrated other providers, the word **"account"** can mean different
things. In Rehive:

- **Wallet** = a balance the customer owns. Rehive *mirrors* it on its ledger:
  Rehive records the balance, it never holds the funds. (What we previously
  called an "Account".)
- **Custody** sits *underneath* a Wallet: Bridge (custodial USDC), Privy
  (end-user self-custody) or your own. The custody layer is where value is
  actually held. Don't call it a "wallet" at our level.
- A **virtual bank account** is a **funding method** for deposits on a Wallet,
  not a top-level object. See [Payments](/money/payments).
