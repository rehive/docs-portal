# The model

The whole API is built from a small set of objects, all in plain product terms.

```
Customer
└─ Account            opened in a mode — what the customer holds
   ├─ Balance         the held value, per asset (available / total)
   ├─ Transfer        one money movement, over a Rail
   └─ Payout          a batch of outbound Transfers

Funding instrument    a way to move money in or out of an Account
Rail                  the route a Transfer takes (method × region × currency)
Event                 emitted for every state change
```

A **Customer** has one or more **Accounts**. An **Account** is opened in a mode
and holds one or more **Balances** (one per asset), along with its **Transfers**
and **Payouts**. Each Transfer moves over a **Rail**; a **Payout** batches
outbound Transfers. **Events** fire on every change.

| Object | What it is | Product |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | A person or business, with onboarding state and verification. | Identity |
| **Account** | What the customer holds — a container opened in a **mode**, with one or more Balances and funding instruments. | Accounts |
| **Balance** | The held value in a single asset within an account (`available` / `total`). ≈ a "wallet" elsewhere. | Accounts |
| **Funding instrument** | A way to move money in or out of an account: a virtual bank account, an on-chain address, or a card. | Accounts |
| **Transfer** | The single primitive for moving value — deposit, withdraw, send, receive, convert. | Accounts |
| **Payout** | A batch of outbound transfers from one account. | Accounts |
| **Rail** | A money-movement route (method × region × currency). Read-only catalogue. | Accounts / Coverage |
| **Policy / Control** | A rule that governs what an account or customer may do. | Compliance |
| **Event** | A state change, delivered to your webhooks. | All |

## A note on terminology

If you've integrated other providers, the word **"account"** can mean different
things. In Rehive:

- **Account** = the *container* you open in a mode.
- **Balance** = the *held value* inside it, per asset (what some providers call a "wallet").
- A **virtual bank account** is a **funding instrument** *on* an account — not a
  top-level object.

See [Terminology](/accounts/funding#terminology) on the Funding page for the
full mapping.
