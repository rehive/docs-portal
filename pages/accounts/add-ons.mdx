# Add-ons

Add-ons ride on an account — switch on what you need, priced per add-on. They're
not separate products; they're capabilities of Accounts.

## Monetize

| Add-on | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fees** | Charge per transaction, by attribute |
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring plans & billing |
| **Payment requests & invoicing** | Request-to-pay into an account |
| **Commerce** | Sell products, run a marketplace or points store |

## Engage

| Add-on | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rewards** | Loyalty & referral campaigns |
| **Notifications** | Email, SMS & push |
| **In-app support** | Messaging & notices |

```sh
curl https://api.rehive.com/v1/accounts/acc_3RtZ0/add-ons \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_..." \
  -d enable="fees,notifications"
```
