# Assets & custody

Accounts isn't locked to stablecoins or one custodian. It flexes on three axes —
which is why it works for everything from a consumer wallet to an enterprise
running its own currency.

## Asset

Use **stablecoins** through our providers, or run your **own currency** — local
fiat, points or tokens — on the multi-asset ledger.

## Custody

| Option | Who holds value | Powered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Custodial** | Managed for your users | Bridge Wallets |
| **Self-custody** | The end user holds their keys | Privy |
| **Held by you** | You hold the value; Rehive is the record | your own treasury |

> "Self-custody" (the user's keys) is distinct from "self-operated"
> infrastructure. We avoid the term "self-hosted custody" because, in regulatory
> language, a *self-hosted wallet* already means a user-controlled one.

## Ledger

- **Held** — track balances and transactions on the ledger.
- **Endpoints-only (pass-through)** — provision [funding instruments](/accounts/funding)
  (virtual accounts, addresses) with **no balance held**; money lands and you
  sweep or forward it.

```sh
  -d mode=usd_account -d ledger=off   # endpoints-only
```
